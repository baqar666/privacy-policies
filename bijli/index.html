Privacy Policy — Bijli Buddy

Effective: 25 September 2026

Bijli Buddy is an unofficial electricity-bill estimation and bill-history tool. It is not affiliated with NEPRA, a DISCO, K-Electric, or the Government of Pakistan.

Information handled

The app has no account system and we do not operate a server that receives your calculator inputs or saved bill history. Bill history and preferences are stored locally on your device and can be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

Official bill websites

The bill-check feature displays or opens the selected electricity provider's website. Information entered there is sent directly to that provider and is governed by its policy; the app does not read or store the form data.

Advertising and updates

The app may show Google AdMob ads. Google may process advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, diagnostics and interaction data to deliver and measure ads. See Google's Privacy Policy. Expo Updates may contact Expo infrastructure to securely deliver app updates and may process technical request data.

Children

The app is a general utility and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information.
