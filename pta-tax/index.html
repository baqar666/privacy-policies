Privacy Policy — PTA Tax Calculator

Last updated: 25 September 2026

PTA Tax Calculator ("the app") is an unofficial tool that estimates PTA/FBR mobile registration taxes. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our own servers. The app has no server. All calculations happen on your device. Your favorites and settings are stored only on your device and never leave it.

IMEI numbers

The app never reads, stores, or transmits your device's IMEI. The IMEI checking feature only opens the official PTA DIRBS website (dirbs.pta.gov.pk), where any information you enter is governed by PTA's own privacy policy.

Advertising and updates

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID) and usage data to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's data practices. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's Google settings. Expo Updates may process technical request data when securely checking for app updates.

Third-party websites

The app links to official government websites (e.g. dirbs.pta.gov.pk). We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those sites.

Children

The app is a general utility and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information.

Disclaimer

This is an unofficial app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to PTA, FBR, or any government body. Tax figures are estimates based on published duty slabs; the actual amount is determined by PTA/DIRBS at registration.
