Privacy Policy — Senpai Says

Effective: 3 November 2026

Senpai Says is a free entertainment app in which original anime-style characters say your name with different emotions. You can play the voice lines, share them, and set them as your ringtone, notification sound or alarm sound.

Summary

The app has no account, no ads, no in-app purchases and no analytics. We do not run a server that receives your information. Everything you enter stays on your phone, except that downloading your name's voice (described below) tells our file host which name file to send.

Information stored on your device

The name you type and your settings (voice language, how the characters address you, theme, animation, daily-message and screen-time preferences) are stored only on your device. You can remove them by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

Downloading your name's voice

The most common names are built into the app. For other supported names, the app downloads a voice pack the first time you use the name, then keeps it on your phone so it works offline. The pack is downloaded over a secure (HTTPS) connection from GitHub Pages, a file hosting service run by GitHub, Inc. The request contains the name's file name (for example en/kainat.zip), and, like any web request, your IP address and basic device information. GitHub may keep this in its server logs under the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not receive these logs and cannot link them to you. If your name isn't supported, nothing is downloaded.

Ringtones, notification and alarm sounds

When you choose to set a voice line as a sound, the app saves that audio file to your device's Ringtones, Notifications or Alarms folder and sets it as the default sound. To do this on Android it asks for the "Modify system settings" permission, which is used only for this purpose. On older Android versions it may also ask for storage permission to save the file.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the app creates an audio file on your device and opens your phone's share menu. The file is sent only to the app or contact you choose, and that service's own privacy policy applies.

Daily messages

If you turn on daily messages, the app schedules local notifications on your device. No notification data is sent to us or to a push service.

Screen time reminders ("Stop me scrolling")

This feature is off unless you turn it on in Settings. When it is on, the app uses Android's "Usage access" permission to count how long you spend each day in the apps you pick (such as Instagram, TikTok or YouTube), and the "Display over other apps" permission to show a break reminder from one of the characters on top of those apps when you pass the daily limit. A small ongoing notification is shown while it is running, and it restarts after your phone reboots so the count keeps going. Only the time spent in the apps you picked is counted; the app does not read what you see or do inside them. This usage information is processed and stored only on your device, is never sent to us or anyone else, and is cleared each day. You can turn the feature off, or remove either permission in your phone's settings, at any time.

Ads, purchases and analytics

This version of the app shows no ads, offers no purchases and includes no analytics or tracking. If a future version adds any of these, we will update this policy before that version is released.

Children

The app is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
