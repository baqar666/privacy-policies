Privacy Policy — Paisa Calculator

Effective: 25 September 2026

Paisa Calculator is an unofficial financial-estimation utility and does not provide tax, legal, lending, or investment advice. It is not affiliated with FBR or any financial institution.

Information handled

The app has no account system and we do not operate a server that receives your salary, loan, committee, zakat, or khums inputs. Calculations run on the device. Committee data, cached public rates and preferences are stored locally and can be removed by clearing app data or uninstalling.

Public rate services

The app requests public gold, silver and USD/PKR rates from gold-api.com and open.er-api.com. These requests may expose routine technical data such as IP address and user agent to those providers, but calculator inputs are not sent.

Advertising and updates

The app may show Google AdMob ads. Google may process advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, diagnostics and interaction data to deliver and measure ads. See Google's Privacy Policy. Expo Updates may process technical request data when checking for updates.

Children

The app is a general utility and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information.
